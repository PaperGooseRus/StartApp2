StartApp2 / PixelSandbox — Windows 10/11 x64

Extract the whole ZIP into a folder. Double-click StartGame.bat.
Keep PixelSandbox.exe and all DLLs together. No compiler is needed to play.
This program has no installer and does not require administrator access.

A/D or arrows: move. Space: jump.
1 sand, 2 water, 3 gas, 4 stone, 5 soil, 6 erase.
Left mouse: paint; right mouse: erase.
P pause; F world speed x30; R reset; F5 save; F9 load; Escape quit.

Diagnostics: double-click CheckGame.bat to test physics, saves and rendering.
You can also open a terminal in this folder and run:
  set SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy
  PixelSandbox.exe --smoke-test
Clear SDL_VIDEODRIVER before launching the visible game.

SDL2 is included under the zlib license; see licenses/SDL2-LICENSE.txt.
This build is unsigned. Windows may show a publisher warning.

Save slot: %APPDATA%\PaperGoose\PixelSandbox\quick-save.pxs
F5 saves the world and player; F9 restores them, including after restart.

Version 0.4: soil absorbs water; wet soil darkens. Hover soil for wetness.
Walk over the dry patch on the left, then water it with brush 2.
F speeds up growth and absorption. Player limbs are visual only for now.
